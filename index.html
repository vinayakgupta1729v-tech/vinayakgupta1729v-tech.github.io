01 / Background

About me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati, where I work with Prof. Sukanta Pati.

I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematics at IIT Madras, under the supervision of Prof. R. Balaji. My research primarily concerns eigenvalues of matrices associated with graphs, especially Laplacian and distance-based matrices, and their connections with combinatorial structure.

My current interests also extend to combinatorial Laplacians of simplicial complexes, spectral inequalities, eigenvalue multiplicities, and extremal problems.

02 / Areas of interest

Research interests

01

Spectral graph theory

Eigenvalue bounds, multiplicities, spectral characterization, and extremal questions for families of graphs.

02

Distance-based matrices

Distance spectra, distance Laplacians, resistance matrices, and their structural information.

03

Simplicial complexes

Higher-dimensional combinatorial Laplacians, face degrees, and spectral properties of complexes.

04

Matrix theory

Matrix inequalities, eigenvalue estimates, and connections to discrete mathematics.

05

Trees and graph families

Structural and spectral problems on trees, threshold graphs, and related classes.

06

Algebraic combinatorics

Using algebraic methods to reveal combinatorial patterns and classification principles.
